Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

We built Morphis to help you be productive — not to collect your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you stay in control.

Data We Collect

In short: Only what's needed to make Morphis work for you.

  • Account info: Your email address (via Apple Sign In) so we can identify your account — that's it.
  • Your productivity data: Focus sessions, expense records, to-do items, and receipts you create. These are stored in our cloud database (Supabase) so you can sync across your devices.
  • Device sensors: Orientation and light sensor data power the skeuomorphic visuals in the app. This data stays on your device — it's never sent anywhere.

Data We Don't Collect

In short: We skip the creepy stuff.

  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers

How We Use Your Data

In short: To make Morphis work for you — nothing more.

  • Syncing your productivity data across your devices
  • Managing your subscription through RevenueCat
  • Keeping your account secure

Third-Party Services

In short: We work with a few trusted partners — here's who and why.

  • Supabase stores your data and handles authentication. Their privacy policy
  • RevenueCat manages in-app purchases so subscriptions work smoothly. Their privacy policy
  • Apple provides Sign In with Apple for secure login. Their privacy policy

Data Retention & Deletion

In short: Your data, your call.

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. Whenever you're ready, head to Settings → Delete Account and we'll permanently remove everything from our servers. No hoops to jump through.

Children's Privacy

In short: Morphis isn't designed for kids under 13.

We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please reach out and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
