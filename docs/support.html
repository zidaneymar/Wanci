Common Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Morphis → Profile → Restore Purchases. Your Pro subscription will be restored automatically if you have an active subscription linked to your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Morphis Pro → Cancel. Your Pro features will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Open Morphis → Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers.

The tuner / metronome isn't picking up sound

Make sure you've granted Morphis microphone access in iPhone Settings → Morphis → Microphone.
